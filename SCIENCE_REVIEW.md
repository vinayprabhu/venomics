## Critique qualifications

- Brand withdrawal, generic approval and local stock availability are different facts. The critiques' exact manufacturer-notification dates were not needed or adopted; official effective withdrawal dates are used.
- The real Byetta photograph retains its wordmark and attribution. Labelling it historical is more faithful than editing an authentic product image into a fictitious generic pen.
- The three citations the first reviewer could not check were verified: the Bothrops primary-duct paper and the current honeybee-extract and Prialt label records.
- The existing honeybee script already described a venom mixture, and the existing cone mechanism already depicted pore blockade. These were clarified rather than described as newly discovered errors.
- Experimental structures are inferred models. Gaussian surfaces, organ travel, anatomical schematics and mechanism loops remain educational representations, not microscopy or molecular-dynamics trajectories. Supplied animal models still lack independent scan/species validation; the existing provenance limitation remains visible in Sources.
